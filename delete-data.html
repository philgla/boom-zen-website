Delete your data — Last updated: October 21, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of the mobile game Boom Zen by Sugar Candy Mountain Gaming (Google Play). The App has no user accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or a password.

How to delete your data

1. Switch off usage analytics. In the game, open the pause menu (gear button) and set “Usage analytics” to OFF. The App stops sending gameplay statistics to Google Firebase Analytics and resets the analytics data and the analytics ID stored on your device.

2. Uninstall the App. All game data (progress, currencies, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Ask us to delete statistics already sent. Send an email to privacy@boom-zen.com with the subject “Boom Zen data deletion”. Because there are no accounts, we can only find your statistics if you help us identify your device; we will tell you what we need and confirm the deletion. If you have already switched analytics off or reinstalled the App, past statistics can no longer be linked to your device; they are deleted automatically after 14 months.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted on request: the gameplay statistics we exported from Firebase Analytics to our own Google Cloud project (BigQuery, data location EU). Without a request they are deleted automatically after 14 months.

Not stored by us: payment details. In-app purchases are processed by Google Play.

Kept by Google under its own rules: purchase records at Google Play and data collected by Google AdMob and Google Firebase, according to their retention periods. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy.
