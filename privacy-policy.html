Privacy Policy — Last updated: October 21, 2026 (analytics outside the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland)

This Privacy Policy describes how Philipp Glatz, trading as Sugar Candy Mountain Gaming ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in connection with the mobile game Boom Zen ("the App"), available on Google Play, and with this website boom-zen.com (see section 8).

1. Information We Collect

The App has no account or login, and we do not ask for or store your name, email address or other directly identifying information. The App uses the following services, which collect certain data automatically, including pseudonymous identifiers (such as the app instance identifier and, where available, the advertising ID). Under the GDPR, such pseudonymous data can be personal data, so this policy describes it in full:

Google AdMob — We use AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads. For users in the EU/EEA, consent is obtained via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised advertising takes place.

Google Firebase Analytics — In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland only if you allow it. There the App asks whether we may collect usage statistics; until you answer, and if you choose “Don't allow”, Firebase Analytics is switched off and sends nothing. Everywhere else Firebase Analytics is switched on from the first launch, and the App shows a notice on the start screen that it collects gameplay statistics and how to switch this off (see section 6). Where you are is determined by Google's User Messaging Platform; until it has answered (e.g. when offline), Firebase Analytics stays off. When analytics is on, we use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the App (e.g. session length, level reached, features used). Firebase uses an app instance identifier and, where available on your device, the advertising ID, and collects usage data; it does not include your name, email or other directly identifying information. To analyse these events, we export them to Google BigQuery in our own Google Cloud project (data location: EU), where they are deleted automatically after 14 months. See Firebase privacy.

Google Play Billing — In-app purchases (e.g. diamond packs and limited-time offers) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. To understand purchasing behaviour, we record (if analytics is on, see above) purchase events (product ID, price, currency, transaction ID, the number of diamonds received and your diamond balance afterwards) in Firebase Analytics, linked to the app instance identifier. See the Google Privacy Policy, which also applies to Google Play.

Reminders (local notifications) — On Android 13 and later the App asks once for permission to show notifications. If you allow it, the App schedules reminders on your device while the App is closed: when your daily diamonds are ready, when a limited-time offer is about to end, and after a few days without playing. These reminders are created and stored only on your device; no push service or server is involved, and no data is sent to create them. Opening the App cancels all scheduled reminders. If analytics is on, we record in Firebase Analytics whether the permission was asked for, granted or denied, and when a reminder is opened. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Boom Zen → Notifications).

Google Play In-App Review — After you have completed a few missions, the App may once ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Whether the dialog actually appears is decided by Google. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; the App does not learn whether or how you rated it. If analytics is on, we only record in Firebase Analytics that the dialog was requested.

2. How We Use Information

Data collected by our third-party partners is used solely to:

— Display relevant advertisements within the App
— Analyse gameplay patterns to improve the App
— Detect technical problems, such as ads or purchases that fail
— Understand purchasing behaviour and complete purchases (credit the diamonds you bought)

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties beyond what is described above.

Legal basis. Personalised advertising, and analytics with Firebase in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and § 25(1) TDDDG for storing and reading information on your device), which you give or refuse in the respective prompt. Outside these regions, analytics with Firebase is based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the App is played in order to fix problems and improve it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object at any time by switching it off in the App (see section 6). Non-personalised advertising is based on our legitimate interest in financing the App as a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Processing of in-app purchases is necessary to perform the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You are not legally or contractually required to provide any of this data; without it, ads may be less relevant and we cannot see how the App is used.

Transfers outside the EU. The services described in section 1 are provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, and Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). Data may therefore be processed in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; for other transfers Google uses the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission.

No automated decisions. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Selecting personalised ads (only with your consent) is done by Google.

3. Children's Privacy

Boom Zen is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. In the EU/EEA, consent to personalised advertising can only be given by users who have reached the age of digital consent in their country (16 in Germany and the Netherlands). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

4. Data Retention

We do not run our own servers. The analytics events we export to Google BigQuery (see section 1) are stored in our Google Cloud project in the EU and deleted automatically after 14 months. Apart from that, data collected by third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) is governed by their respective privacy policies and retention periods.

Deletion. Switching off analytics in the App (see section 6) resets the analytics data on your device; uninstalling the App removes all game data stored on your device. Because the App has no account, we do not know who you are and can only find analytics events about you if you help us identify your device. To request deletion of these events before the 14 months have passed, contact us at privacy@boom-zen.com.

Security. All data is sent from the App to the Google services over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to our Google Cloud project and the analytics data is restricted to the developer.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (this does not affect processing before the withdrawal), and the right to object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw your analytics consent directly in the App (see section 6) or by contacting us at privacy@boom-zen.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For the analytics events in our BigQuery project (see section 4), contact us at the address above. For data held by the third-party providers, you can also contact them directly:

— Google AdMob and Google Play (in-app purchases): policies.google.com/privacy
— Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Your Choices: Personalised Ads and Analytics

Personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

— Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Additionally, a consent prompt is shown at first launch for users in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game via Pause menu → Privacy settings.

Analytics. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland the App asks for your consent (“Help us make Boom Zen better?”) after your first mission. Everywhere else analytics is on from the first launch and the start screen shows a notice about it; you have the right to object at any time by switching it off. You can change your choice at any time in the game via Pause menu → Usage analytics. When switched off, the App stops sending gameplay and purchase events to Firebase Analytics, stops Firebase data collection on your device and resets the analytics data stored on your device. You can switch it on again at any time in the same place.

7. Third-Party Links

The App contains the following links to external content:

— Privacy policy (Pause menu → Privacy policy) opens this page in your browser.
— Rate Boom Zen (Pause menu) opens the Boom Zen page in the Google Play Store, where you can leave a rating.
— Share Boom Zen (Pause menu) opens the share menu of your device with a short text and a link to the Boom Zen store page. You choose the app and the recipients yourself. The App does not send anything itself and does not learn whom you share with.

The Google Play Store and the app you share with are governed by their own privacy policies. Any third-party services used by the App are listed in this policy.
