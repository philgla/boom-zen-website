BOOM ZEN

Privacy Policy — Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Sugar Candy Mountain Gaming ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Boom Zen ("the App"), available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

Google AdMob — We use AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads. For users in the EU/EEA, consent is obtained via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised advertising takes place.

Google Firebase Analytics — We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the App (e.g. session length, level reached, features used). Firebase uses an app instance identifier and collects usage data; it does not include your name, email or other directly identifying information. See Firebase privacy.

Google Play Billing — In-app purchases (e.g. diamond packs) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. To understand purchasing behaviour, we record purchase events (product ID, price, currency, transaction ID and the number of diamonds received) in Firebase Analytics, linked to the app instance identifier. See the Google Privacy Policy, which also applies to Google Play.

2. How We Use Information

Data collected by our third-party partners is used solely to:

— Display relevant advertisements within the App
— Analyse gameplay patterns to improve the App
— Monitor app stability and performance
— Understand purchasing behaviour and verify purchases

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties beyond what is described above.

3. Children's Privacy

Boom Zen is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

4. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Any data collected by third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) is governed by their respective privacy policies and retention periods.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. Since we do not directly hold personal data, requests should be directed to the relevant third-party providers:

— Google AdMob and Google Play (in-app purchases): policies.google.com/privacy
— Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Opt-Out of Personalised Ads

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

— Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Additionally, a consent prompt is shown at first launch for users in the EU/EEA. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game via Pause menu → Privacy settings.

7. Third-Party Links

The only external link in the App opens this Privacy Policy (Pause menu → Privacy policy). Any third-party services used are listed in this policy.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
