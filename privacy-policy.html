BOOM ZEN

Privacy Policy — Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Philipp Glatz, trading as Sugar Candy Mountain Gaming ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in connection with the mobile game Boom Zen ("the App"), available on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store any personally identifiable information. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain data automatically:

Google AdMob — We use AdMob to display rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads. For users in the EU/EEA, consent is obtained via the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalised advertising takes place.

Google Firebase Analytics — We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players interact with the App (e.g. session length, level reached, features used). Firebase uses an app instance identifier and, where available on your device, the advertising ID, and collects usage data; it does not include your name, email or other directly identifying information. To analyse these events, we export them to Google BigQuery in our own Google Cloud project (data location: EU), where they are deleted automatically after 14 months. See Firebase privacy.

Google Play Billing — In-app purchases (e.g. diamond packs and limited-time offers) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. To understand purchasing behaviour, we record purchase events (product ID, price, currency, transaction ID, the number of diamonds received and your diamond balance afterwards) in Firebase Analytics, linked to the app instance identifier. See the Google Privacy Policy, which also applies to Google Play.

Reminders (local notifications) — On Android 13 and later the App asks once for permission to show notifications. If you allow it, the App schedules reminders on your device while the App is closed: when your daily diamonds are ready, when a limited-time offer is about to end, and after a few days without playing. These reminders are created and stored only on your device; no push service or server is involved, and no data is sent to create them. Opening the App cancels all scheduled reminders. We record in Firebase Analytics whether the permission was asked for, granted or denied, and when a reminder is opened. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Boom Zen → Notifications).

Google Play In-App Review — After you have completed a few missions, the App may once ask Google Play to show its rating dialog. Whether the dialog actually appears is decided by Google. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play; the App does not learn whether or how you rated it. We only record in Firebase Analytics that the dialog was requested.

2. How We Use Information

Data collected by our third-party partners is used solely to:

— Display relevant advertisements within the App
— Analyse gameplay patterns to improve the App
— Detect technical problems, such as ads or purchases that fail
— Understand purchasing behaviour and complete purchases (credit the diamonds you bought)

We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data with third parties beyond what is described above.

Legal basis. Personalised advertising is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give or refuse in the consent prompt. Analytics with Firebase and non-personalised advertising are based on our legitimate interest in improving the App and financing it as a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Processing of in-app purchases is necessary to perform the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3. Children's Privacy

Boom Zen is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

4. Data Retention

We do not run our own servers. The analytics events we export to Google BigQuery (see section 1) are stored in our Google Cloud project in the EU and deleted automatically after 14 months. Apart from that, data collected by third-party services (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) is governed by their respective privacy policies and retention periods.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data, and the right to object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR), for example analytics. You can object to analytics directly in the App (see section 6) or by contacting us at privacy@boom-zen.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For the analytics events in our BigQuery project (see section 4), contact us at the address above. For data held by the third-party providers, you can also contact them directly:

— Google AdMob and Google Play (in-app purchases): policies.google.com/privacy
— Google Firebase: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Opt-Out of Personalised Ads and Analytics

Personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

— Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation

Additionally, a consent prompt is shown at first launch for users in the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game via Pause menu → Privacy settings.

Analytics. You can switch off analytics at any time in the game via Pause menu → Usage analytics. When switched off, the App stops sending gameplay and purchase events to Firebase Analytics, stops Firebase data collection on your device and resets the analytics data stored on your device. You can switch it on again at any time in the same place.

7. Third-Party Links

The App contains the following links to external content:

— Privacy policy (Pause menu → Privacy policy) opens this page in your browser.
— Rate Boom Zen (Pause menu) opens the Boom Zen page in the Google Play Store, where you can leave a rating.
— Share Boom Zen (Pause menu) opens the share menu of your device with a short text and a link to the Boom Zen store page. You choose the app and the recipients yourself. The App does not send anything itself and does not learn whom you share with.

The Google Play Store and the app you share with are governed by their own privacy policies. Any third-party services used by the App are listed in this policy.
